Image Format Practice

The browser picks the best format (WebP) and the right size for the screen.

What I did

  • <picture> with a WebP <source> and a JPEG fallback.
  • srcset and sizes, so a phone downloads the small 400px image and a laptop the 800px one.
  • width, height and alt on the image.
  • How to check: DevTools, then the Network tab, then Img. Reload the page and look at the type (webp) and size.
