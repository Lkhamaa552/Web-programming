Tteokgalbi Recipe

What You Need

  • 500g groundbeef
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper

Steps

  1. Chop the onion and garlic finely.
  2. Mix the ground beef
  3. Warning: use medium heat when cooking the meat.
  4. Shape the mixture into small round patties.
  5. Add sesame oilbefore serving
